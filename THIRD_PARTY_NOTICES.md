## Important distinction for brand assets

An open-source license covering an icon repository does not necessarily grant permission to imply endorsement, misuse a trademark, or ignore a brand owner's usage guidelines. Review the relevant brand rules when publishing logos or favicons in a product.

This file is a practical notice, not legal advice. The authoritative terms are the upstream license files and brand-owner policies.
